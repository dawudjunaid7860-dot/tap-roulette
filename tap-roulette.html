❓ How to Play

Super simple — no menus, no sign-up, no waiting. Just tap and play.

1
Open it — you're already playing 🎉

The second Tap Roulette opens, a random game is loaded for you. No start screen.

2
Every game is 1 minute ⏱️

Watch the timer at the top. When it hits 0:00, a brand-new random game auto-spins in. Timed games now run the full 60 seconds too.

3
Don't want to wait? Spin! 🎲

Hit the big yellow SPIN NEW GAME button anytime for an instant new random game.

4
Beat your best 🏆

Your score shows on the game card. Every game keeps a Best — try to smash it before the minute runs out.

5
Replay a favorite 🕒

Tap History (top right) to see everything you've played, with a Play Again button on each one — plus all 8 games to pick from.
